The interactive network is designed to serve both phonological and philosophical purposes. One of the central challenges in the study of Wei phonology is the limitation of available data. Wang Bi’s rhymed commentaries, however, contribute 7,157 end rhymes, significantly expanding the phonological dataset for the Wei period, and this interactive network connects these end rhymes based on their coexistence within the same rhyme group, potentially enabling phonologists to better digest the data and analyze the rhyme system. It highlights connections between key terms in Wang Bi’s commentaries that fall within the web of rhymes but does not require prior phonological knowledge to further explore potential form-meaning relationships. The lines connecting end rhymes in the network—and their relative lengths—indicate the frequency of co-occurrence, thereby suggesting a phonological and conceptual “hierarchy.” For instance, although “merit” (gong 功 /*-ung/) rhymes with “wisdom” (sheng 聖 /*-eng/), its phonological relationship with sheng 聖 is less proximate than that between sheng 聖 and “brightness” (ming 明 /*-eng/) as well as other terms closely associated with sheng at the center of the network. While the conceptual hierarchy may be less robust than the phonological one—given that the network includes only those terms employed by Wang Bi as end rhymes—the ultimate goal is to visualize the complex relationship among those rhymed terms and to build a bridge between form and meaning in Wang Bi’s works in particular and between phonological and philosophical approaches more broadly.
